\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Data and protocol.} The public Criteo conversion logs released
with~\cite{chapelle2014modeling}. Clicks span 60 days; days $[0,30)$ initialise the model
causally and days $[30,60)$ are the streaming period, divided into \nCohorts{} hourly rounds
giving \nEval{} scored clicks at a base conversion rate of \baseRate{}. Learning rates are
selected per arm and per cell from a seven-point grid on a held-out slice of the initialisation
period, before any streaming evaluation, and pinned across seeds. The teacher's rate is fixed
and decoupled from the student's, so the student's sweep tunes one model rather than two.

\paragraph{Axes.} Two \emph{attribution windows}, $v=1$\,d and $7$\,d, standing for a fast and a
slow funnel; the contrast between them is the paper's point, so we never average across them.
Three \emph{join delays}, $o\in\{5,30,90\}$ minutes. Five \emph{served capacities} from
\capMin{} to \capMax{} parameters and \flopMin{}--\flopMax{} MFLOPs per inference --- the two do
not track, the smallest rungs differing 5.2$\times$ in parameters at identical compute, so we
report both. \seedsCore{} seeds on the three targets and \seedsOther{} elsewhere.

\paragraph{Two campaigns, one axis.} The evidence is \nRunsBoth{} runs: \nRunsThree{} over
\nArmsThree{} arms reproducing the published methods, and \nRunsFour{} over \nArmsFour{} arms
studying the three targets at the larger teacher. The second uses a frozen copy of the first's
harness, and \textsc{Wait} --- which instantiates no teacher, so nothing the second campaign
changed can reach it --- reproduces the first to zero difference across all 150 shared cells.
Reporting every arm against \textsc{Wait} within its own run is what puts both campaigns on one
axis, rather than an assumption that two runs are comparable.

\paragraph{Metric and statistics.} Normalised entropy against $\Yv$, lower better, pooled over
all scored clicks rather than averaged over rounds --- a per-round average normalises each hour
by its own base rate and discards diurnal variation a model can legitimately predict. We report
the relative reduction against \textsc{Wait} within a cell, computed per seed and then averaged.
Seeds differ only in initialisation and minibatch order, so seed $i$ of two arms is a matched
pair: paired $t$-test within each cell, Holm--Bonferroni across the \nCells{} cells of a
contrast, counting only cells whose \emph{gain} is positive and survives. Dispersion is small
away from \textsc{Fresh} (median $\NE$ sd \seedSdMed{}, maximum \seedSdMax{}); \textsc{Fresh}
reaches \seedSdFresh{} at $o=5$ minutes, which is a result rather than a nuisance. Seeds measure
initialisation variance on one data origin: precision, not generalisation.
