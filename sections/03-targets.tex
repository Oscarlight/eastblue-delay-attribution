\section{Four Targets}
\label{sec:targets}

Writing the record early leaves a deficit: conversions arriving between $o$ and $v$ are recorded
as negatives, and something must stand in for them in the record's single frozen target. We
study four answers. All four share a pipeline, schedule, feature set and optimiser, and differ
only in the target $\tilde y_i$ and in the age $\Delta$ at which the record is written.

\textsc{Fresh} writes at $\Delta=o$ with $\tilde y_i=\Yo_i$, filling the deficit with nothing.
\textsc{Wait} writes at $\Delta=v$ with $\tilde y_i=\Yv_i$, avoiding the deficit by deferring the
write. It has a correct label on data of age $v$, and it is the conventional per-objective
pipeline, so it is our baseline throughout. A practitioner who refuses both to duplicate records
and to train on wrong labels is already running it.

\textsc{Correct-Distill} writes at $\Delta=o$, keeps the observed label and softens only the
observed negatives:
\begin{equation}
  \tilde y_i \;=\; \Yo_i + (1-\Yo_i)\,\wt(x_i),
  \qquad
  \wt(x) \approx P\!\left(\Yv{=}1 \,\middle|\, \Yo{=}0,\, x\right).
  \label{eq:hybrid}
\end{equation}
An observed positive stays exactly $1$, and only the uncertain mass moves. This is the
functional form used by \textsc{Ulc}~\cite{wang2023unbiased}; the two differ in how $\wt$ is
estimated, so comparing them isolates the estimator and nothing else.

\textsc{Distill-Only} goes further and regresses on $\pv(x)\approx P(\Yv{=}1\mid x)$ alone, so
the record carries features and a number but no observation at all.

\paragraph{The teacher.}
The estimates $\wt$ and $\pv$ come from a separate model trained on the \emph{matured} stream,
in which a record enters at $C_i+v$ carrying $\Yv_i$. Both quantities are read out from one
shared trunk. The teacher is \emph{evaluated} at the instant the student's record is written:
at $C_i+o$ the student queries it and freezes the returned number into the record. Rule~1 is
therefore satisfied even though the target is not binary. Softness is a different way of
satisfying causality, not an exemption from it. The construction is distillation from privileged
information~\cite{xu2019privileged}, in which the teacher observes the matured label that the
student by construction cannot.

Being used only during training, the teacher costs nothing at serving time, though it does mean
served capacity is matched across arms while total training capacity is not. We therefore treat
teacher architecture as an experimental axis in Section~\ref{sec:setup}.

We also distinguish two ways of maintaining the teacher. A \emph{continuously retrained} teacher
keeps consuming the matured stream throughout the run, so deploying it means operating a second
training job alongside the first. A \emph{fixed} teacher is trained once during causal
initialisation and never updated, so deploying it means shipping a static model file. The
distinction is invisible in Eq.~\eqref{eq:hybrid} and decisive operationally, so we measure
both.

Finally, \textsc{Oracle} writes at $\Delta=o$ but labels the record $\Yv_i$. It violates Rule~1
and is not deployable. We include it because it bounds what any causal correction could recover,
which is what turns a positive result into a statement about remaining headroom.
