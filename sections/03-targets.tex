\section{What Fills the Gap}
\label{sec:targets}

Shortening $o$ leaves a gap: conversions arriving between $o$ and $v$ are recorded as negatives,
and something must stand in for them in the record's single frozen target. We study four answers,
identical in pipeline, schedule, features and optimiser, differing only in the target
$\tilde y_i$ and the age $\Delta$ at which the record is written.

\textsc{Fresh} ($\tilde y_i=\Yo_i$ at $\Delta=o$) fills the gap with nothing. \textsc{Wait}
($\tilde y_i=\Yv_i$ at $\Delta=v$) avoids having one by deferring the write: correct label,
$v$-old data. It is the segregated status quo and our baseline throughout --- a practitioner who
refuses both to duplicate records and to train on wrong labels is already running it.
\textsc{Correct-Distill} writes at $\Delta=o$ and softens only the observed negatives,
\begin{equation}
  \tilde y_i \;=\; \Yo_i + (1-\Yo_i)\,\wt(x_i),
  \qquad
  \wt(x) \approx P\!\left(\Yv{=}1 \,\middle|\, \Yo{=}0,\, x\right),
  \label{eq:hybrid}
\end{equation}
so an observed positive stays exactly $1$ and only the uncertain mass moves. This is the
functional form of \textsc{Ulc}~\cite{wang2023unbiased}; what differs is the estimator, so a
comparison between them isolates that and nothing else. \textsc{Distill-Only} goes further and
regresses on $\pv(x)\approx P(\Yv{=}1\mid x)$ alone: the record carries features and a number,
and no observation at all.

\paragraph{The teacher.} A separate model trained on the \emph{matured} stream --- a record
enters it at $C_i+v$ carrying $\Yv_i$ --- with $\pv$ and $\wt$ as read-outs on one trunk. It is
\emph{evaluated} at the write instant: at $C_i+o$ the student queries it and freezes the number
into the record, so Rule~1 holds even though the target is soft: softness is
another way of satisfying causality, not an exemption from it. The construction is distillation
from
privileged information~\cite{xu2019privileged}: the teacher sees the matured label, exactly what
the student cannot.

Teacher capacity is \teacherParams{} parameters (\teacherFlops{} MFLOPs). Being training-only
it costs nothing at serving time, but it does mean \emph{served} capacity is matched across arms
while total training capacity is not --- which is why teacher size is an experimental axis rather
than a fixed detail. Nor need the teacher be live: ours keeps learning from the matured stream,
making it a service, whereas frozen after causal initialisation it is a build-time artifact. We
run both. \textsc{Oracle}, writing at $\Delta=o$ but labelling the record $\Yv_i$, violates
Rule~1 and bounds what any correction could recover.
