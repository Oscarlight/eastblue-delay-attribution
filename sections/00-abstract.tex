Conversion pipelines are split by speed: an upper-funnel onsite conversion arrives in minutes, a
down-funnel offsite one in days. Each objective's \emph{attribution window} --- what counts as a
conversion --- is conventionally also its \emph{join delay}, the age at which its training record
is written, so every objective needs its own pipeline and is stale by its own window. We ask
whether the two can be separated: can a long-window objective learn from records joined within
90 minutes?

Joining sooner records every in-flight conversion as a negative, and a single-visit stream, which
trains each click at most once, cannot repair it later --- yet most published corrections do
exactly that: of \nScreened{} methods we reproduce, \nFail{} write more than one record per click,
up to \maxDup{}. The gap must instead be closed at write time, in production by a training-only
foundation model such as GEM, here by a \teacherParams{}-parameter teacher.

On the Criteo Delayed Feedback Dataset, across five served capacities (\capMin{}--\capMax{}
parameters, \flopMin{}--\flopMax{} MFLOPs), three join delays and \seedsCore{} seeds, we compare
three targets: the matured label (Wait), the teacher's estimate alone (Distill-Only), and the
observed label with only its negatives softened (Correct-Distill). Against a Wait baseline of
$\NE$ \waitNeMin{}--\waitNeMax{}, Distill-Only at one day lowers $\NE$ by \doOneSmallAbs{}\% at
the two smallest capacities but raises it by \doOneLargeAbs{}\% at the three largest. Correct-Distill
beats Distill-Only in all \nCellsBoth{} configurations and Wait in \cdBeatWait{}, lowering $\NE$
by \cdSevenMinAbs{}--\cdSevenMaxAbs{}\% on the seven-day task and \cdOneMeanAbs{}\% on average
on the one-day task --- \recoverSeven{}\% and \recoverOne{}\% of what a label-peeking oracle recovers.
The gain grows as the funnel slows and needs no larger teacher: a \teacherRatio{}$\times$ larger
teacher and a \wideFlopRatio{}$\times$ more expensive one each move it by under
\teachDoAbs{}\,pp. Decoupling the two delays removes the main obstacle to training objectives
with different attribution windows on one fresh stream.
