\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Conversion pipelines are split by speed. An upper-funnel onsite conversion --- a view, an
add-to-cart --- is observable within minutes of the click; a down-funnel offsite one, a purchase
or an app install, arrives after a day or more. Each objective waits out its own
\emph{attribution window} before its training record exists, and conventionally that window is
also its \emph{join delay}: the age at which the record is written. Equating the two forces
every objective onto its own pipeline and makes each stale by exactly the width of the window it
waited for.

A slow objective therefore trains on week-old data, and consolidating several objectives into
one model inherits the worst of every window (Sect.~\ref{sec:consolidation}). We ask whether the
two delays can be separated: can an objective accountable to a seven-day window be trained from
records joined within 90 minutes?

Joining sooner is not free. A record written at age $o$ records every conversion still in flight
as a negative, and the deficit is large --- at $o=5$ minutes only \foOneFive{} of a one-day
window's positives have arrived, and \foSevenFive{} of a seven-day window's. A substantial
literature corrects this
bias~\cite{chapelle2014modeling,yasui2020feedback,yang2021capturing,chen2022asymptotically,ding2026delayed},
but most corrections repair the record after the fact --- duplicating the event when its outcome
lands, relabelling it, adding heads at further horizons --- and a pipeline that trains each click
at most once can do none of these. We make that restriction explicit as a \emph{single-visit}
constraint and measure who obeys it: of \nScreened{} published methods reproduced under one
protocol, \nFail{} write more than one record per distinct click, up to \maxDup{}
(Sect.~\ref{sec:setting}).

What is left is a choice of target. If the record carries one frozen number, the gap between
what is observed and what the objective means must be closed at write time by an estimate --- in
production by a large model trained across objectives on matured outcomes, a foundation model
such as GEM~\cite{meta2025gem}, attractive because it already exists and is not refitted per
objective. Here we stand in for it with a \teacherParams{}-parameter training-only teacher and
compare three targets across five served capacities, three join delays and two attribution
windows. The correction
works, the gain grows as the funnel slows, it needs no larger teacher --- and it is conditional
on the record keeping part of its hard label.
