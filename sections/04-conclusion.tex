\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An objective's attribution window is fixed by the business definition of a conversion; the join
delay its training pipeline adopts is not. We have shown that the two can be set independently
without paying for it. Writing the record within minutes and replacing only its observed
negatives with a teacher's estimate beats waiting for the matured label in \cdBeatWait{} of
\nCellsBoth{} configurations and recovers \recoverSeven{}\% of what a label-peeking oracle
attains at seven days, with the gain growing as the funnel slows, and it does so under the
single-visit constraint that most published corrections violate. Which target to write is a
function of the served capacity: a small model can drop the observed label entirely and train
on the teacher's estimate alone, while a larger one should keep the observed positive. The
correction is cheap to adopt, being insensitive to the teacher's architecture and still
effective when the teacher is frozen after initialisation and shipped as a static file.

The next step is consolidating several ranking objectives into one model and one stream, an
active industrial direction~\cite{meta2025gem} whose first obstacle is a pipeline obstacle
rather than a modelling one. Under \textsc{Wait}, objective $k$'s record exists only at
$C_i+v_k$, so a shared append-only stream must either write $K$ records per click, violating
single-visit $K$ times over, or delay every objective until $\max_k v_k$ and impose the slowest
funnel's staleness on the fastest. The target studied here removes that dilemma in principle: a
single stream at one short join delay can carry
$\tilde y_{i,k}=\Yo_{i,k}+(1-\Yo_{i,k})\,\wt_k(x_i)$ for every objective at once, because
$\wt_k$ is a function the pipeline evaluates rather than an outcome it awaits, and the teachers
sit upstream as static files rather than $K$ running training jobs. We ran one objective at a
time, so the open question is interference: whether $K$ heads sharing a trunk and $K$ soft
targets of differing sharpness still train stably. Settling it needs $K$ prediction heads, $K$
attribution windows, one stream, and a production-scale teacher, whose richer features should
also sharpen the corrector beyond what a corpus of anonymised categorical fields allows.
