\section{Concluding Remarks}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An objective's attribution window is fixed by the business definition of a conversion; the join
delay its pipeline adopts is not. Setting the two independently improves accuracy rather than
degrading it, recovering \recoverSeven{}\% of what a label-peeking oracle attains at seven days
and helping most where the funnel is slowest. Which target to write depends on the capacity
served: above the smallest rungs the record should keep its observed positive, while at small
capacity the label can be dropped and the teacher's estimate carried alone.

The consequence is for training several ranking objectives in one model, an active industrial
direction~\cite{meta2025gem} whose first obstacle is a pipeline obstacle rather than a modelling
one. Under \textsc{Wait}, objective $k$'s record exists only at $C_i+v_k$, so a shared
append-only stream must either write $K$ records per click, violating Rule~2 $K$ times over, or
delay every objective until $\max_k v_k$. A single stream at one short join delay instead
carries $\tilde y_{i,k}=\Yo_{i,k}+(1-\Yo_{i,k})\,\wt_k(x_i)$ for every objective at once,
because $\wt_k$ is a function the pipeline evaluates rather than an outcome it awaits.
\textsc{Ulc} reaches comparable accuracy, so the choice between them is architectural:
\textsc{Ulc} fits each $w_k$ inside the served model's training loop, whereas a teacher places
each upstream with a single number written onto a record as its only interface.
Section~\ref{sec:exp} shows that upstream model need not be retrained while the student trains,
so consolidation adds $K$ static files to the build rather than $K$ running training jobs.

Two limits bound the claim. We ran one objective at a time, so we have no evidence on
interference between objectives sharing a trunk, which is the central risk in the target above,
and one corpus with one chronological split measures precision rather than generalisation. The
experiment this makes worth running is $K$ prediction heads, $K$ attribution windows, one
stream, and the teachers placed upstream.
