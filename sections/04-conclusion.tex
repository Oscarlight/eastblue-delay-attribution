\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An objective's attribution window is fixed by the business definition of a conversion; the join
delay its training pipeline adopts is not. We have shown that the two can be set independently. 
Writing the record within minutes and replacing only its observed
negatives with a teacher's estimate beats waiting for the matured label in \cdBeatWait{} of
\nCellsBoth{} configurations and recovers \recoverSeven{}\% of what a label-peeking oracle
attains at seven days, and it does so under the
single-visit constraint that most published corrections violate. The
correction is cheap to adopt, being insensitive to the teacher's architecture.

The next step is consolidating several ranking objectives into one model and one stream, an
active industrial direction~\cite{meta2025gem} whose first obstacle is one of pipelines rather
than of modelling. Under \textsc{Wait}, objective $k$'s record exists only at
$C_i+v_k$, so a shared append-only stream must either write $K$ records per click, violating
single-visit $K$ times over, or delay every objective until $\max_k v_k$ and impose the slowest
funnel's staleness on the fastest. The target studied here removes that dilemma in principle: a
single stream at one short join delay can carry
$\tilde y_{i,k}=\Yo_{i,k}+(1-\Yo_{i,k})\,\wt_k(x_i)$ for every objective at once. 
In future work, we will generalise this to $K$ prediction heads, $K$
attribution windows, one stream, and a production-scale teacher model.
