\section{Setting and the Single-Visit Constraint}
\label{sec:setting}

A click $i$ occurs at $C_i$ with features $x_i$ and, if it converts, after a delay $D_i>0$
($D_i=\infty$ otherwise). The \emph{attribution window} $v$ belongs to the objective and fixes
the target $\Yv_i=\mathbf{1}[D_i\le v]$, knowable only at $C_i+v$; the \emph{join delay} $o$
belongs to the pipeline and decides when the record is written, carrying
\begin{equation}
  \Yo_i \;=\; \mathbf{1}\!\left[D_i \le o\right] \;\le\; \Yv_i .
  \label{eq:labels}
\end{equation}
Setting $o=v$ is the segregated status quo; $o\ll v$ is what we want, and the inequality is its
price. Write $F(o)=P(D\le o\mid D\le v)$ for the share of the target's positives visible at write
time. Training runs in hourly rounds --- consume the records released since the last cut-off,
then score every click of the next hour once, retrospectively against $\Yv$ --- so every arm is
judged on the business label whatever it trained on, and update-then-predict keeps the evaluation
causal.

Two rules govern what a deployable system may do. \textbf{Rule~1 (causality):} a record's label
must be computable when the record is written and must not change afterwards. Labelling a record
$\Yv$ while writing it at $C_i+o$ uses an outcome that has not happened --- an upper reference
(\textsc{Oracle}), not a system. \textbf{Rule~2 (single-visit):} with $n_i$ the number of times
click $i$ enters the served model's loss over its lifetime, $\max_i n_i\le 1$. Without it
shortening $o$ is trivial --- write early, write again later. The criterion is the maximum rather
than the mean, since it is the \emph{existence} of a second write that obliges the pipeline to
support one, and it governs the \emph{served} stream: charging training-only auxiliaries too
would eliminate \textsc{Fsiw}, \textsc{Ulc} and our own arms alike.

records each one's served model consumes per distinct click, \nFail{} write more than
one.\footnote{The denominator must be \emph{distinct clicks touched}: dividing records consumed
by records released conflates duplication with traffic variation. We also score only clicks whose
whole revisit horizon lies inside the stream, since a shorter window cannot see the revisit.}
