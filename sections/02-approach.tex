\section{Problem Setting and Proposed Approach}
\label{sec:approach}

A click $i$ occurs at time $C_i$ with features $x_i$ and, if it converts, does so after a delay
$D_i>0$, with $D_i=\infty$ when it never converts. The attribution window $v$ belongs to the
objective and fixes the target $\Yv_i=\mathbf{1}[D_i\le v]$, knowable only at $C_i+v$. The join
delay $o$ belongs to the pipeline and fixes when the record is written, so the label available
at that moment is
\begin{equation}
  \Yo_i \;=\; \mathbf{1}\!\left[D_i \le o\right] \;\le\; \Yv_i .
  \label{eq:labels}
\end{equation}
Setting $o=v$ reproduces the conventional per-objective pipeline. Setting $o\ll v$ is what we
want, and the inequality in Eq.~\eqref{eq:labels} is its price. We write
$F(o)=P(D\le o\mid D\le v)$ for the fraction of the objective's conversions already visible when
the record is written. Training proceeds in hourly rounds, each consuming the records released
since the previous cut-off and then scoring every click of the following hour exactly once
against the matured target $\Yv$. Every method is therefore judged on the business label
whatever it trained on, and updating before predicting keeps the evaluation causal.

\paragraph{Two rules a deployable pipeline must satisfy.}
\textbf{Rule 1, causality.} A record's label must be computable at the moment the record is
written, and must not change afterwards. Assigning $\Yv_i$ to a record written at $C_i+o$ uses
an outcome that has not yet occurred. Such an arm is an upper reference, not a system, and we
report it under the name \textsc{Oracle}. \textbf{Rule 2, single-visit.} Let $n_i$ be the number
of times click $i$ enters the served model's loss over its lifetime. Rule~2 requires
$\max_i n_i\le 1$. Without it the question is empty, since any join delay can be shortened by
writing a record early and writing it again later. We take the maximum over clicks rather than
the mean, because it is the existence of a second write that obliges the pipeline to support
revision, and the rule governs the served stream only: charging training-time auxiliaries would
eliminate \textsc{Fsiw}, \textsc{Ulc} and our own arms alike.

\paragraph{Which published methods comply.}
We reproduced \nScreened{} published methods from scratch under a single protocol and counted
the records each one's served model consumes per distinct click. \nFail{} write more than
one.\footnote{Two measurement traps both understate duplication. The denominator must be
distinct clicks touched, since dividing records consumed by records released conflates
duplication with variation in click volume. And a scoring window shorter than a method's revisit
horizon cannot observe the revisit, so we score only clicks whose entire horizon lies inside the
stream.} At $v=1$\,d the counts are \input{generated/screen_inline}. The highest,
\maxVisitsArm{}, reaches \maxVisits{} writes for a single click, its mechanism being an update
applied whenever a label reverses. The plain re-ingestion
baseline~\cite{ktena2019addressing,gu2021real} measures \vanillaDup{} and fails the same
criterion. Duplication is a property of emission logic rather than of the model, and the audit
returns identical counts at the smallest and largest served capacities. Only \passList{} satisfy
Rule~2. Of these, \textsc{Fsiw} corrects the loss and \textsc{Twice} adds a prediction head on
the conversion clock, both leaving the target itself untouched. \textsc{Ulc} alone changes the
target, which makes it our closest comparator.

\paragraph{Four targets.}
What remains is a choice of what the single frozen target should contain. We study four answers,
sharing a pipeline, schedule, feature set and optimiser, and differing only in the target
$\tilde y_i$ and in the age $\Delta$ at which the record is written. \textsc{Fresh} writes at
$\Delta=o$ with $\tilde y_i=\Yo_i$, filling the deficit with nothing. \textsc{Wait} writes at
$\Delta=v$ with $\tilde y_i=\Yv_i$, avoiding the deficit by deferring the write. It has a correct
label on data of age $v$, and it is the conventional per-objective pipeline, so it is our
baseline throughout. A practitioner who refuses both to duplicate records and to train on wrong
labels is already running it. \textsc{Correct-Distill} writes at $\Delta=o$, keeps the observed
label and softens only the observed negatives:
\begin{equation}
  \tilde y_i \;=\; \Yo_i + (1-\Yo_i)\,\wt(x_i),
  \qquad
  \wt(x) \approx P\!\left(\Yv{=}1 \,\middle|\, \Yo{=}0,\, x\right).
  \label{eq:hybrid}
\end{equation}
An observed positive stays exactly $1$, and only the uncertain mass moves. This is the functional
form used by \textsc{Ulc}~\cite{wang2023unbiased}; the two differ in how $\wt$ is estimated, so
comparing them isolates the estimator and nothing else. \textsc{Distill-Only} goes further and
regresses on $\pv(x)\approx P(\Yv{=}1\mid x)$ alone, so the record carries features and a number
but no observation at all. Finally, \textsc{Oracle} writes at $\Delta=o$ but labels the record
$\Yv_i$. It violates Rule~1 and is not deployable. We include it because it bounds what any
causal correction could recover, which is what turns a positive result into a statement about
remaining headroom.

\paragraph{The teacher.}
The estimates $\wt$ and $\pv$ come from a separate model trained on the \emph{matured} stream,
in which a record enters at $C_i+v$ carrying $\Yv_i$. Both quantities are read out from one
shared trunk. The teacher is \emph{evaluated} at the instant the student's record is written: at
$C_i+o$ the student queries it and freezes the returned number into the record. Rule~1 is
therefore satisfied even though the target is not binary. Softness is a different way of
satisfying causality, not an exemption from it. The construction is distillation from privileged
information~\cite{xu2019privileged}, in which the teacher observes the matured label that the
student by construction cannot. Being used only during training, the teacher costs nothing at
serving time, though it does mean served capacity is matched across arms while total training
capacity is not, so we treat teacher architecture as an experimental axis. We also distinguish
two ways of maintaining it. A \emph{continuously retrained} teacher keeps consuming the matured
stream throughout the run, so deploying it means operating a second training job alongside the
first. A \emph{fixed} teacher is trained once during causal initialisation and never updated, so
deploying it means shipping a static model file. The distinction is invisible in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:hybrid} and decisive operationally, so we measure both.
