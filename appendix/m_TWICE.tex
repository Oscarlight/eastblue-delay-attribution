\subsection{\textsc{Twice}}\label{app:m-TWICE}

\paragraph{Definition.} \textsc{Twice}~\cite{li2026twice} keeps its CVR tower on a single fresh record and
   routes conversion-clock arrivals to a separate delay head, so the served tower stays
   single-visit. The delay head reads the conversion clock, which is why its R2 status is
   boundary rather than clean.

\begin{equation}
\tilde y_i = \Yo_i \ \text{ plus a separate delay head on the conversion clock}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Compliance.} Measured at $1.00$ training records per click (maximum 1) over the 720-hour replay. Single-visit; admissible under Rule~2.

\paragraph{Architecture.} The served model is the A0--A4 ladder of Table~\ref{tab:spec-ladder}, unchanged. Auxiliary: delay head, ReLU MLP $[64,32]$, no shared parameters, which does \emph{not} scale with the rung, so the capacity axis measures the served model only.
