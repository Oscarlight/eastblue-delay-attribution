\subsection{\textsc{Oracle}}\label{app:m-Oracle}

\paragraph{Definition.} Not causal and not deployable: the fresh record is labelled with an outcome that had
   not arrived when it was written. It exists to bound the recoverable gap. Sect.~\ref{sec:hardness}
   shows it is \emph{not} an upper bound at small capacity, which is the paper's most surprising
   result.

\begin{equation}
\tilde y_i = \Yv_i \ \text{ emitted at } \Delta=o
\end{equation}
