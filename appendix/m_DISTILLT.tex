\subsection{\textsc{Wait}}\label{app:m-DISTILL-T}

\paragraph{Definition.} Emit nothing until the window closes, then write one record carrying the true label.
   The label is correct and Rule~1 is satisfied at $\Delta=v$, but the freshest data the model has
   ever seen is $v$ old. This is the admissible production status quo and the anchor for
   $\mathrm{RI}^{\textsc{wait}}$.

\begin{equation}
\tilde y_i = \Yv_i \ \text{ emitted at } \Delta=v
\end{equation}
